aria-er · open data

Air quality in Emilia-Romagna, as JSON.

The marts of aria-er, exported every morning by the pipeline itself: exceedances of the legal limits, trends, traffic against background, weather and data completeness, from ARPAE open data.

Version 1 of the contract.

See them as charts in the case study →

Files (data/v1)

  • meta.json — refresh time, period covered, rows per file, credits
  • exceedances.json — days or hours over each legal limit, per station and year
  • pollutant-trend.json — annual mean per municipality and pollutant
  • traffic-vs-background.json — monthly traffic and background means in the same municipality
  • weather-correlation.json — PM10 against wind and rain, per station and season
  • completeness-monthly.json — data coverage per station, pollutant and month

Columns, units and time zone: the contract. Readable from any origin (CORS open).
